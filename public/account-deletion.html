DoseEase Account & Data Deletion

At DoseEase, we respect your privacy and data autonomy. In full compliance with Google Play Store User Data policies, this page provides instructions on how you can delete your DoseEase account and all associated personal health data.

Important Warning: Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. All registered medications, scheduled reminder times, adherence history logs, caregiver connection links, and profile details will be permanently purged from all systems.

Method 1: Delete Directly Inside the App (Instant)

  1. Open the DoseEase app on your device.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Tap the Profile & Settings tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  4. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap the red Delete Account button.
  5. Confirm your decision in the prompt. Your data will be deleted immediately and your session terminated.

Method 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have access to the DoseEase application, you can submit an account and data deletion request by contacting our privacy team:

  • Email: privacy@doseease.app
  • Subject Line: DoseEase Account Deletion Request
  • Information to provide: The registered email address associated with the account you wish to delete.

Requests submitted via email are verified and processed within 48 hours. A final confirmation email is sent once all records have been completely purged.

Data Types Permanently Purged

  • User Account Credentials (Email, Name, Hash, Phone Number)
  • All Prescriptions, Strengths, Formats, and Label Images
  • Full Historical Adherence Records and Confirmation Timestamps
  • Caregiver Links and Access Tokens
  • Custom Alert and Notification Preferences
